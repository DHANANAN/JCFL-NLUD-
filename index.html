About

About the Journal

The Journal of Corporate and Financial Laws (JCFL) is a peer-reviewed academic journal published by the Centre for Corporate Law, Governance & Financial Laws (CCLGFL) at National Law University Delhi. JCFL serves as the Centre's flagship publication for original, rigorous, and policy-relevant legal scholarship.

The Journal publishes doctrinal, empirical, and comparative research across corporate law, financial regulation, insolvency, securities, corporate governance, competition, FinTech, ESG, and commercial law. JCFL welcomes contributions from academics, practitioners, judicial officers, and advanced research scholars.

All submissions undergo a double-blind peer review by subject-matter specialists. The Journal is published under a Diamond Open Access model — free to read, free to publish. All citations follow the Bluebook 21st Edition standard.

Learn More →

Double-Blind Peer Review

Every manuscript is reviewed by at least two independent subject-matter scholars with full identity anonymisation on both sides.

Diamond Open Access

No article processing charges for authors. All content freely accessible worldwide. Authors retain copyright.

Bluebook 21st Edition

All submissions must conform to the Bluebook 21st Edition citation standard throughout.

Institutional Profile

Centre for Corporate Law, Governance & Financial Laws

The Centre for Corporate Law, Governance & Financial Laws (CCLGFL) at National Law University Delhi is a specialised institutional hub dedicated to rigorous empirical and doctrinal legal research, policy analysis, and scholarly dialogue at the confluence of corporate governance, capital markets, insolvency regimes, and financial regulation.

The Centre's work is guided by a vision to cultivate a premier national platform for legal scholarship that informs corporate jurisprudence, guides financial regulatory reform, and deepens understanding of market governance in emerging and developed economies.

Scholarly Research

Peer-reviewed publications, working papers, and doctrinal monographs on corporate and financial law.

Policy Engagement

Evidence-based memos and consultation responses to SEBI, MCA, RBI, NCLT, and Parliamentary Committees.

Academic Conclaves

National symposia and international colloquia convening jurists, regulators, and leading academics.

Student Fellowships

Mentoring student scholars through the Editorial Board and research assistantship programme.
