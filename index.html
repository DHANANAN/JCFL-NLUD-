About

About the Journal

The Journal of Corporate and Financial Laws (JCFL) is a peer-reviewed academic journal published by the Centre for Corporate Law, Governance & Financial Laws (CCLGFL) at National Law University Delhi. JCFL serves as the Centre's flagship publication for original, rigorous, and policy-relevant legal scholarship.

The Journal publishes doctrinal, empirical, and comparative research across corporate law, financial regulation, insolvency and restructuring, securities, corporate governance, competition, FinTech, ESG, and commercial law. JCFL welcomes contributions from academics, legal practitioners, judicial officers, and advanced research scholars.

JCFL is committed to the highest standards of academic publishing. All submissions undergo a double-blind peer review by subject-matter specialists. The Journal is published under a Diamond Open Access model, ensuring that scholarship reaches the widest possible readership without financial barriers to authors or readers. All citations follow the Bluebook 21st Edition standard.

Learn More About JCFL →

Double-Blind Peer Review

Every manuscript is reviewed by at least two independent subject-matter scholars. Author and reviewer identities are concealed throughout the review process.

Diamond Open Access

JCFL is published under a Diamond Open Access model. There are no article processing charges for authors, and all content is freely accessible to readers worldwide.

Bluebook 21st Edition

All submissions must conform to the Bluebook 21st Edition citation standard. Detailed author guidelines are provided in the submission documentation.

Institutional Profile

Centre for Corporate Law, Governance & Financial Laws

The Centre for Corporate Law, Governance & Financial Laws (CCLGFL) at National Law University Delhi is a specialised institutional hub dedicated to rigorous empirical and doctrinal legal research, policy analysis, and scholarly dialogue at the confluence of corporate governance, capital markets, insolvency regimes, and financial regulation.

As corporations navigate transformative shifts—from artificial intelligence in boardrooms to fractional securities tokenisation, cross-border insolvency under UNCITRAL, and BRSR sustainability obligations—CCLGFL provides analytical precision and principled guidance for policymakers, courts, and practitioners.

The Centre's work is guided by a commitment to cultivating a premier national platform for legal scholarship that informs corporate jurisprudence, guides financial regulatory reform, and deepens understanding of market governance in emerging and developed economies.

Scholarly Research

Peer-reviewed publications, working papers, and doctrinal monographs on corporate and financial law.

Policy Engagement

Evidence-based memos and consultation responses to SEBI, MCA, RBI, NCLT, and Parliamentary Committees.
