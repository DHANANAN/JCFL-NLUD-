Governance & Academic Leadership

Editorial Board & Advisory Council

The academic, faculty, and student editorial leadership of the Journal of Corporate and Financial Laws.

The Journal of Corporate and Financial Laws is established under the scholarly aegis of National Law University Delhi. The Editorial Board exercises independent academic discretion in accordance with international Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) guidelines and double-blind peer review protocols.

Constitution of the Editorial Board

The student editorial board is selected through a competitive, merit-based annual evaluation process open to senior undergraduate and postgraduate law students at National Law University Delhi. For editorial board inquiries or nominations to the Advisory Council, please write to submissions.jcfl@institution.ac.in.
