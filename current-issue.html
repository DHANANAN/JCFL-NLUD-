Articles: 6 peer-reviewed contributions

✓  Diamond Open Access

The inaugural edition of the Journal of Corporate and Financial Laws presents six peer-reviewed contributions examining foundational and frontier questions in Indian and comparative corporate law: algorithmic governance, cross-border insolvency, tokenized securities, digital competition regulation, sustainability disclosure, and contested corporate control.

Articles in This Issue

  1. Article  ·  Corporate Governance · Artificial Intelligence

    Algorithmic Boardrooms and the Business Judgment Rule: Reimagining Director Fiduciary Duties in the Era of Autonomous Corporate Systems

    Dr. Arundhati Sen & Vikramaditya Nair

    Examines whether the classical business judgment rule can meaningfully insulate directors who rely upon black-box computational analytics for capital allocation and strategic M&A valuation, proposing an augmented standard of 'algorithmic procedural due diligence'.

    pp. 1–38 · DOI: 10.1000/jcfl.2026.01.01
    Read →

  2. Article  ·  Insolvency & Restructuring · International Law

    Harmonizing Cross-Border Insolvency: Institutional Readiness and Creditor Priority Under the Proposed UNCITRAL Adaptation

    Prof. Raghavendra Joshi

    Critically assesses the draft framework adapting the UNCITRAL Model Law on Cross-Border Insolvency, examining COMI jurisprudence, the public policy carve-out, and creditor hierarchy before the NCLT.

    pp. 39–72 · DOI: 10.1000/jcfl.2026.01.02
    Read →

  3. Essay  ·  Securities Regulation · FinTech

    Fractional Capital and Tokenized Securities: Regulatory Perimeter of SEBI and the Digital Asset Conundrum

    Ananya Deshmukh

    Interrogates SEBI's CIS regulations, SM-REIT amendments, and custody mandates as applied to fractional tokenization models.

    pp. 73–98 · DOI: 10.1000/jcfl.2026.01.03
    Read →

  4. Article  ·  Competition & Markets · Digital Regulation

    From Ex-Post Penalties to Ex-Ante Prescriptions: Evaluating the Draft Digital Competition Bill and Systemically Significant Digital Enterprises

    Siddharth Chawla

    Evaluates proposed ex-ante obligations for Systemically Significant Digital Enterprises under India's Draft Digital Competition Bill, with reference to the EU Digital Markets Act.

    pp. 99–134 · DOI: 10.1000/jcfl.2026.01.04
    Read →

  5. Note  ·  ESG & Sustainability

    Greenwashing in the Capital Markets: BRSR Core Verification, ESG Rating Provider Accountability, and the Limits of Voluntary Corporate Disclosures

    Meera Krishnan

    Analyses SEBI's BRSR Core framework and the registration regime for ESG Rating Providers, examining legal remedies against greenwashing under securities fraud provisions.

    pp. 135–158 · DOI: 10.1000/jcfl.2026.01.05
    Read →

  6. Case Comment  ·  Mergers & Acquisitions

    Creeping Control, Interlocking Shareholdings, and the Fiduciary Duties of Target Boards Under the SEBI Takeover Code

    Devendra Singhania

    Examines recent contested control battles before the SAT and Supreme Court, analysing the neutrality rule under Regulation 26 of the SEBI Takeover Code.

    pp. 159–182 · DOI: 10.1000/jcfl.2026.01.06
    Read →
