Journal — Scope

Research Areas

Thematic areas of scholarship published in the Journal of Corporate and Financial Laws.

JCFL publishes doctrinal, empirical, and comparative scholarship across twelve defined research areas. Interdisciplinary contributions integrating law with economics, finance, corporate sociology, or public policy are particularly encouraged, provided they maintain rigorous legal doctrinal framing.

01

Corporate Law

Theoretical, statutory, and comparative examinations of corporate entity structure, director duties, shareholder primacy, and capital maintenance.

Director DutiesShareholder ActivismCorporate PersonalityCapital StructuringDerivative Actions
02

Corporate Governance

Evaluations of board independence, institutional investor stewardship, audit committee integrity, and executive compensation architecture.

Independent DirectorsBoard StewardshipExecutive RemunerationRelated Party Transactions
03

Securities Regulation

Critical analysis of primary and secondary market frameworks, disclosure regimes, insider trading prohibitions, and listing norms.

SEBI ICDR RegulationsInsider TradingPublic OfferingsMarket Manipulation
04

Banking & Finance

In-depth research on prudential regulation, credit facilities, syndicated lending, securitization, and systemic risk mitigation.

Prudential NormsSyndicated LendingAsset ReconstructionPayment Systems
05

Insolvency & Restructuring

Doctrinal and empirical scholarship on corporate insolvency resolution, liquidation priority, cross-border insolvency, and creditor rights.

IBC 2016Creditor CommitteesCross-Border InsolvencyPre-Packaged Restructuring
06

Competition & Markets

Antitrust enforcement, merger control thresholds, abuse of dominant position, cartels, and competition in digital market ecosystems.

Merger ControlAbuse of DominanceDigital GatekeepersCartel Leniency
07

Financial Regulation

Macroprudential policies, shadow banking oversight, international financial centres, and systemic stability mechanisms.

IFSC GIFT CityShadow BankingDerivatives RegulationSystemic Risk
08

Commercial Law

Contractual risk allocation, commercial dispute mechanisms, international sale of goods, warranties, and secured commercial transactions.

Commercial ContractsSpecific ReliefCommercial ArbitrationSupply Chain Liability
09

Emerging Business Regulation

Legal frameworks governing startups, venture capital, founders' agreements, dual-class shares, and platform economy regulation.

Venture CapitalDual-Class VotingStartup GovernanceGig Economy
10

FinTech & Digital Finance

Regulatory sandboxes, tokenized securities, central bank digital currencies, algorithmic trading, and decentralized finance oversight.

CBDC ArchitectureAlgorithmic TradingP2P LendingSmart Contracts
11

ESG & Corporate Responsibility

Business responsibility and sustainability reporting, green bonds verification, supply chain due diligence, and climate litigation.

BRSR FrameworksGreenwashingClimate GovernanceSustainable Financing
12

Mergers & Acquisitions

Hostile takeovers, scheme of arrangements, squeeze-outs, private equity buyouts, and fiduciary considerations in control transactions.

SEBI Takeover CodeNCLT Merger ApprovalsPE BuyoutsDissenting Shareholders
