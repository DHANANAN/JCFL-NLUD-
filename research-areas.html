Thematic Scope & Editorial Jurisdiction

12 Defined Areas of Scholarly Inquiry

Thematic domains within the publication mandate of the Journal of Corporate and Financial Laws.

JCFL solicits and publishes doctrinal, empirical, comparative, and interdisciplinary legal scholarship across twelve defined core disciplines. Contributions integrating law with financial economics, corporate governance theory, or public policy are particularly welcomed provided they maintain rigorous legal doctrinal grounding.

01

Corporate Law

Theoretical, statutory, and comparative examinations of corporate entity doctrine, director duties under Section 166, shareholder primacy, capital maintenance, and derivative litigation.

Director Duties Shareholder Activism Corporate Personality Capital Maintenance
02

Corporate Governance

Evaluations of independent director oversight, institutional investor stewardship, audit committee efficacy, executive remuneration architecture, and related party transactions.

Independent Directors Board Stewardship Executive Pay Related Party Deals
03

Securities Regulation

Critical analyses of primary and secondary market frameworks, SEBI ICDR and LODR regulations, insider trading prohibitions, disclosure standards, and market manipulation enforcement.

SEBI Regulations Insider Trading Public Offerings Market Integrity
04

Banking & Finance

In-depth research on prudential regulation, syndicated loan facilities, asset reconstruction companies (ARCs), project finance contracts, and systemic liquidity frameworks.

RBI Prudential Norms Syndicated Lending Asset Reconstruction Credit Facilities
05

Insolvency & Restructuring

Doctrinal and empirical scholarship on corporate insolvency resolution under the IBC, 2016, committee of creditors supremacy, liquidation priority hierarchies, pre-packs, and UNCITRAL cross-border models.

IBC 2016 Creditor Priority Cross-Border Insolvency NCLT Jurisprudence
06

Competition & Markets

Antitrust enforcement, merger control thresholds, abuse of dominant position, cartel leniency regimes, and ex-ante digital gatekeeper regulation under the Draft Digital Competition Bill.

Merger Control Abuse of Dominance Digital Gatekeepers CCI Enforcement
07

Financial Regulation

Macroprudential systemic stability, shadow banking oversight, international financial services centres (GIFT City IFSC), and OTC derivatives clearing frameworks.

GIFT City IFSC Shadow Banking Derivatives Macroprudential Policy
08

Commercial Law

Complex contractual risk allocation, specific relief jurisprudence, commercial court procedures, warranties and indemnities, and commercial arbitration enforcement.

Commercial Contracts Commercial Courts Arbitration Contractual Risk
09

Emerging Business Regulation

Legal frameworks governing early-stage ventures, private equity investments, founders' agreements, dual-class voting structures, and gig-economy business models.

Venture Capital Dual-Class Voting Startup Governance Platform Economy
10

FinTech & Digital Finance

Regulatory sandboxes, tokenized real estate and securities, Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) architecture, algorithmic trading oversight, and decentralized protocols.

Tokenized Securities CBDC Architecture Algorithmic Trading Smart Contracts
11

ESG & Corporate Responsibility

Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting (BRSR Core), ESG Rating Provider liabilities, green bond certification standards, and supply chain environmental diligence.

BRSR Core Framework Greenwashing Remedies ESG Rating Providers Sustainable Finance
12

Mergers & Acquisitions

Takeover regulation, target board neutrality under Regulation 26 of the SEBI Takeover Code, schemes of arrangement before the NCLT, private equity buyouts, and dissenting shareholder appraisals.

SEBI Takeover Code Target Board Fiduciary Duty NCLT Mergers Creeping Acquisitions
