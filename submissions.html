Call for Papers

The Journal of Corporate and Financial Laws (JCFL) invites original, unpublished scholarly manuscripts from legal academics, legal practitioners, judicial officers, and advanced research scholars across India and internationally.

JCFL publishes doctrinal, empirical, and comparative scholarship across its twelve defined research areas. Manuscripts are accepted on a rolling basis. Contributors are advised to review the submission requirements below prior to submission.

Submission email: submissions.jcfl@institution.ac.in

Manuscript Types & Word Limits

JCFL accepts the following categories of manuscripts. All word counts are exclusive of footnotes, abstract, and title page.

CategoryDescriptionWord Limit
ArticlesFull doctrinal, empirical, or comparative research papers6,000 – 12,000 words
EssaysAnalytical or opinion-based scholarly essays3,000 – 6,000 words
Case CommentsCritical commentary on significant judicial decisions or regulatory orders2,500 – 4,000 words
Book ReviewsScholarly review of recently published legal texts1,200 – 2,500 words

Manuscript Preparation

  • Manuscripts must be submitted in Microsoft Word (.docx) format.
  • Font: Times New Roman, 12pt. Line spacing: 1.5. Margins: 1 inch on all sides.
  • Citations must conform strictly to Bluebook 21st Edition standards throughout.
  • An abstract of 150–250 words must be included on the title page.
  • A list of 5–8 keywords must be provided immediately following the abstract.
  • The title page must include the manuscript title, author name(s), affiliation, designation, and contact email. This information must not appear anywhere else in the document (for blind review purposes).
  • All footnotes should be placed at the foot of the page. Endnotes are not accepted.
  • Tables, figures, and charts must be in editable format, numbered sequentially, and titled.
  • Manuscripts must not be simultaneously submitted to or published in any other journal.

Peer Review Process

JCFL operates a double-blind peer review process. The identities of authors and reviewers are not disclosed to each other at any stage of the review process.

  1. Initial Screening — The Editorial Board conducts an initial review for conformance with submission requirements, scope relevance, and minimum scholarly standards. Submissions that do not meet basic requirements are returned without peer review.
  2. Desk Review — Submissions that pass initial screening are assessed by the Editor-in-Chief or a Section Editor for thematic fit and potential contribution.
  3. Blind Review — Each manuscript is sent to at least two independent peer reviewers with relevant subject-matter expertise. Reviewers complete a structured evaluation form and provide written recommendations.
  4. Editorial Decision — Following receipt of reviewer reports, the Editorial Board issues one of the following decisions: Accept, Minor Revision, Major Revision, or Reject.
  5. Revision & Final Acceptance — Where revisions are required, authors are given a specified period to revise and resubmit. Revised manuscripts may undergo a further round of review.

JCFL aims to provide an initial decision within six weeks of complete submission. This timeline is indicative and may vary.

Publication Ethics

JCFL is committed to upholding the highest standards of academic integrity and publication ethics. Authors, reviewers, and editors are expected to comply with the following obligations.

Authors: Authors must ensure that all submitted work is original. Plagiarism in any form — including self-plagiarism — is unacceptable. Authors must disclose any conflicts of interest and must accurately cite all sources.

Reviewers: Reviewers must maintain strict confidentiality of all manuscripts under review and must disclose any conflicts of interest that could influence their review.

Editors: Editors must evaluate manuscripts solely on the basis of academic merit and scope relevance, without regard to the authors' identity, affiliation, nationality, or institutional rank.

Allegations of misconduct will be investigated by the Editorial Board in accordance with established academic publishing standards.

AI & Academic Integrity Policy

JCFL acknowledges that authors may use AI tools as research aids. The following policy governs AI use in submissions:

  • AI tools cannot be listed as authors. Authorship requires human accountability for the intellectual content of the work.
  • Where AI language tools have been used in the drafting, editing, or summarisation of manuscript content, authors must disclose this use in the acknowledgements section, specifying the tool and the nature of its use.
  • The accuracy of all legal citations, quotations, and factual claims generated or assisted by AI tools remains the sole responsibility of the human author(s).
  • Manuscript submissions that consist substantially of AI-generated text will be rejected.

Open Access Policy

The Journal of Corporate and Financial Laws is published under a Diamond Open Access model.

  • All published articles are freely accessible to readers worldwide, with no subscription fees or paywalls.
  • There are no Article Processing Charges (APCs) for authors. Publication in JCFL is free of charge.
  • Published articles are licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0) licence, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Authors retain copyright in their work. By submitting to JCFL, authors grant JCFL and CCLGFL, NLU Delhi a non-exclusive right to publish and disseminate the work.
