About CCLGFL

Centre for Corporate Law, Governance & Financial Laws — National Law University Delhi

The Centre

Centre for Corporate Law, Governance & Financial Laws

The Centre for Corporate Law, Governance & Financial Laws (CCLGFL) at National Law University Delhi is a specialised institutional hub for rigorous empirical and doctrinal legal research, policy analysis, and scholarly dialogue at the intersection of corporate governance, capital markets, insolvency regimes, and financial regulation.

Established within one of India's premier national law universities, the Centre serves as an interdisciplinary platform for bringing together legal academics, regulatory practitioners, financial economists, and corporate jurists to advance scholarship with direct relevance to India's evolving corporate and financial legal landscape.

The Centre's flagship publication, the Journal of Corporate and Financial Laws (JCFL), is a peer-reviewed, Diamond Open Access academic journal that publishes original scholarship across twelve defined research areas in corporate and financial law.

Vision

To cultivate a premier national platform for legal scholarship that informs corporate jurisprudence, guides financial regulatory reform, and deepens understanding of market governance in emerging and developed economies.

Mission

The Centre advances its mission through four interlocking pillars: (i) publication of rigorous peer-reviewed scholarship; (ii) evidence-based policy engagement with regulatory bodies; (iii) convening academic and professional exchanges; and (iv) developing the next generation of corporate and financial legal scholars through student fellowships and mentorship.

Centre Activities

What the Centre Does

Scholarly Publications

Publishing peer-reviewed articles, essays, case comments, and working papers in corporate and financial law through the Journal of Corporate and Financial Laws (JCFL).

Policy Engagement

Preparing evidence-based legal memoranda and consultation responses for SEBI, MCA, RBI, NCLT, and Parliamentary Standing Committees on financial legislation.

Academic Conclaves

Organising national symposia and international colloquia convening senior jurists, regulatory officials, industry practitioners, and leading legal academics.

Student Fellowships

Mentoring student scholars through the JCFL Editorial Board and Centre research assistantship programme, fostering the next generation of corporate legal thinkers.

Working Paper Series

Disseminating early-stage research for peer feedback through a pre-publication working paper series covering emerging developments in corporate and financial law.

Comparative Research

Engaging with scholars and institutions internationally to advance comparative perspectives on common law and civil law corporate governance regimes.
