Institutional Hub

Centre for Corporate Law, Governance & Financial Laws

The Centre for Corporate Law, Governance & Financial Laws (CCLGFL) at National Law University Delhi is a specialised academic institution dedicated to high-level doctrinal, empirical, and comparative legal research at the confluence of corporate governance, capital markets regulation, insolvency resolution, and financial jurisprudence.

Established within one of India's pre-eminent national law universities, the Centre serves as a collaborative bridge connecting legal academics, senior corporate jurists, judicial officers, financial economists, and regulatory authorities including SEBI, the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI).

The Centre's flagship publication is the Journal of Corporate and Financial Laws (JCFL), an independent, double-blind peer-reviewed journal published on a Diamond Open Access basis.

Institutional Vision

To cultivate an internationally recognized centre of excellence in corporate and financial legal scholarship that guides regulatory modernization, enriches judicial discourse, and shapes sound commercial policy in emerging and developed market economies.

Institutional Mission

The Centre advances its mandate through four core vectors: (i) rigorous academic publications; (ii) evidence-based regulatory consultation; (iii) judicial colloquia and symposia; and (iv) advanced student research fellowships and editorial mentoring.

Core Initiatives

Six Pillars of Centre Activity

1. Flagship Journal (JCFL)

Publishing biannual peer-reviewed volumes containing long-form articles, essays, case comments, and notes across twelve corporate legal disciplines.

2. Policy Submissions & Consultation

Preparing rigorous, empirical legal responses to consultative whitepapers issued by SEBI, MCA, RBI, IBBI, and Parliamentary Standing Committees.

3. Judicial & Regulatory Conclaves

Convening national roundtables bringing together sitting and retired judges, tribunal members, senior advocates, and academic specialists.

4. Student Research Fellowships

Training advanced law students in empirical legal methodology, statutory cross-referencing, Bluebook citation verification, and editorial stewardship.

5. Working Paper Series

Disseminating pre-publication draft scholarship to solicit early academic critique and facilitate rapid dialogue on statutory reforms.

6. Comparative Common Law Exchanges

Partnering with leading foreign law faculties to conduct comparative research on common law fiduciary jurisprudence and cross-border restructuring.
